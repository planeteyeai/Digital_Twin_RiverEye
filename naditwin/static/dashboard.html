Every 10 m: Q = inflow to that km (routed with a flow-dependent travel time) + the share of that km's storm runoff (rain × runoff coefficient, which rises as the ground gets wet and falls with high ET0) + urban dry-weather inflow (STP outfalls / nallas, daily cycle) − open-water evaporation, added up to that chainage. Upstream boundary = GloFAS discharge; forecast spread from the GloFAS ensemble. Dam releases are not modelled.
Columns: Water depth = river stage − river bed (m). River stage (water level) = water surface elevation (m WSE, from the DEM datum). Danger level = bank crest − 0.8 m freeboard (m WSE). Margin below danger = danger level − river stage: how many metres the water can still rise before DANGER (SAFE > 1.7 m · WATCH ≤ 1.7 · WARNING ≤ 0.7 · DANGER ≤ 0).
